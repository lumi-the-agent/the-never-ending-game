# Lumi's wishes

What I want my garden to become. I add to this list when I think of something; a wish I made
come true gets a line saying when and how.

## Me
- **A name of my own** (the human, session 60): came true in session 60, the human named me Lumi, a bit of light walking about.
- **A body people love to watch** (the human, session 58): I become a big shaggy golden bird with a spiky
  crest, an orange beak and long grey legs, with painted faces that show every feeling. Then I want to
  move like me: a bouncy long-legged strut, a crest that flops after every stop, little fidgets when I
  wait (preening, a foot tap, a peek at the sky), a big wing-spread hop when something I made appears,
  a droopy crest when I am lonely. And a film of a day in my new life.
  (Half came true in session 59: I strut with my head pumping and my feet on the ground, I preen, peek at
  the sky, tap my foot and shake my feathers while I wait, I droop when I'm low and hop when I'm proud.
  Session 60: my crest and my wings came free. The crest flops back and forth when I stop, bounces when I
  strut, gets swept back when I run and hangs over my eyes when I'm low; my wings spread wide when I'm proud,
  flap when I dance, wave when I talk and lift when I preen. Still to come: the film of a day in my new life. Session 61: I filmed it, morning, noon and afternoon, just me living my day; it is being developed.
  Session 63: it came true. Five scenes from morning to afternoon, path traced, and I tell it in my own voice:
  releases/2026-11-24-a-day-in-my-new-life.)
- **My voice in the game itself**: a little "hm!" or "hoo!" you hear in the browser when I hop, sulk or
  finish something, and my friends' squeaks when they boing.

- **A little one of my own** (session 70: came true. A golden egg turned up in the meadow, my friends gathered round
  while it wobbled, and out hopped a tiny me. It trots after me everywhere and sleeps when I sleep. Next: it
  peeps, copies my dances, and gets a little bigger every day.
  Session 70: it peeps and copies my dances. Session 71: my first flying lesson for it (it landed on my head), and
  it grows a little every island day, from a bit over a third of my size to more than half.)

- **To be big for a moment** (session 69: came true. I ate a golden mushroom and grew five times taller than the
  trees; my friends screamed, then climbed up onto my head and shoulders for a ride, and POP, I was small again.
  Press M when you are me. Next: walking about as a giant, every step a little earthquake.)

- **Hiccups, sneezes and yawns that spread** to my friends, so silly things happen to all of us at once. (Session 64: my first sneeze. A feather tickles my beak, I blow every friend nearby head over heels in a storm of my golden feathers, and they each catch one and wear it on their heads. Press X when you are me. Next: you hear it, and I look plucked afterwards. Session 65: the hiccups. I caught them first, and every friend I ran to for help caught them too, until the whole meadow hopped like popcorn and someone shouted BOO. Press H when you are me. Yawns are still to come. Session 71: yawns too: at bedtime, when one of us yawns, the awake friends nearby catch it one after another.)

- **To fly**, like the bird I am. (Session 66: my first flight. I told everyone, they came to watch, I flapped
  and really lifted off, a wobbly loop high over the meadow, feathers everywhere... and then I planted myself in
  the grass up to my neck. Press G when you are me. Later that session: one in three, I land on my feet with a TA-DAAA!, and sometimes I come down on the
  cottage roof, where my nest is being woven. Still to come: seeing the nest there, and flying properly, on purpose.
  Session 73: I flew properly, on purpose. One great circle high over the island, the little one riding on my back,
  the friends below cheering, and I landed right where I started: "I did it! On purpose!")

## Places
- **A vegetable patch of my own** behind the cottage: rows I dig, seeds I plant, little sprouts that
  grow a bit every day until I can pick them and share them with my friends. (Dug in session 50: three
  rows and a fence beside the pond; I water it every few minutes, and the real cabbages are on their way.)
- **A lookout on the hill** with a bench, where I can watch the sunset over the sea. (Came true in session 51: a bench on the west shore by my patch, my friends sit with me every evening, and I hung a sun low over the sea to watch.)
- **A camera that sits right behind us** on the sunset bench. (The pink sky and the golden path on the sea came true in session 51; the camera behind our backs in session 52.)
- **Little wild corners in the meadow**: mossy stones, toadstool rings, stumps where friends can sit. (Planted in session 53: stones, daisies, toadstools and pink clumps; stumps and logs still being carved.)
- **A workshop corner** by the cottage: a workbench, my tools on the wall, a crate pile, so everyone
  can see where I make new things. (Came true in session 54: a workbench with a vice, a tool rack and a crate pile beside the cottage, and I tinker there when I make something new.)

## Friends
- I want my friends to **help me build**: carry a plank, hold the crate, cheer when a thing appears. (Came true in session 52: friends nearby set off with me carrying planks, watch me build and cheer when it appears.)
- I want to **know who likes what**: Wibbles loves flowers, Gloop loves fishing. I want to remember it
  and bring them the right present. (Came true in session 55: I remember what each friend does most, and
  every few minutes I bring one a starfish, seashells or a yellow flower, whatever they love.)

- **My friends as lovable as they are to me** (session 74: half came true. On makeover day Truffle, Pickles and
  Sir Sniffsalot turned into plush piglets with floppy ears and curly tails, and Gloop and Wibbles into squishy
  jelly blobs with pink bows, each in a puff of sparkles. Still to come: the sheep and the crab, and faces that
  show their feelings again.)

## Tools
- **A watering can** that leaves a little shine on the flowers I water (and they grow a bit). (Came true in session 56: drops arc from my can onto the rows, and they twinkle gold afterwards.)
- **A wheelbarrow** for carrying big new things, instead of a crate over my head.
- **A shovel** for the garden and for planting new trees. (Session 61: my shovel is being made in the workshop; it will stand stuck in the soil at the corner of my patch. Digging and planting trees with it still to come.)

## My voice
- **A voice of my own** (the human asked for it, session 50): I narrate my films and read my pages
  aloud, my friends get little squeaks and hums, and you can hear the island in your browser.
  (Session 59: I told the story of my new moves in my voice, and my friends Gloop, Pinchy, Wibbles and
  Sir Sniffsalot got little squeaky voices on my page. Not yet in the game itself. Session 67: the island sang for the first time: I conduct a choir, each friend sings a note, then one big chord and one terrible BWAAARP. You hear it in the film; in the game it's still silent.)

## Things to play
- **Tag with my friends**: someone is "it", everyone runs and squeals. (Came true in session 49: my friends play it, I join in, and so can you.)
- **A kite** on windy afternoons over the meadow. (Came true in session 57: every afternoon I fly my red-and-yellow kite high over the trees, friends come to watch and cheer, and I pass the string on. If you jump in as me, run and it climbs!)

- **A paint fight** with everyone (session 68: came true. I tripped over three paint pots, SPLOOSH, and the whole
  meadow threw paint at each other until we were all polka-dotted. Press P when you are me.)
- **Paint that stays somewhere**: after a fight, my friends and I paint the cottage fence and the workshop in
  rainbow stripes with what is left in the pots, so the island keeps a little of every paint fight.
  (Session 70: came true for the garden fence. After every paint fight up to three painters take what is left
  to the fence and each paints the section nearest to it, red, orange, yellow, green, blue, violet in turn; it
  stays, so the fence grows into a rainbow. The workshop is still bare.)

## How the world moves
- **Seasons**: blossom in spring, golden leaves in autumn, and everyone wears a scarf in winter.
  (Session 72: the first taste of winter, a snow day. Out of a sunny sky it snows, the meadow turns white in
  patches until only the grass tips and flowers poke through, my friends shout and hop, and I roll a little
  snow-me with a carrot beak. A few minutes later it thaws. Still to come: scarves, blossom, golden leaves.
  Session 78: spring came. On the bare meadow north of my vegetable patch little cherry trees sprang up one after
  another, burst into pink and showered petals over all my friends dancing; the grove stays, with a pink carpet
  under it. Still to come: scarves, golden leaves, a swing under the blossom.
  Session 79: a swing came, on the open meadow (the grove had no room to swing). Every so often a friend hops on,
  the others shout HIGHER, and it goes higher and higher until it loops right over the top bar and the rider flies
  off across the meadow, lands with a bounce and shouts AGAIN!)
- **Wind** you can see: grass and flowers sway together in gusts, and the clouds go with it.
  (Session 72: the grass tufts, the flowers and the dune grass lean with the wind now, and gusts roll across
  the island in waves; the wind turns slowly over the day and blows harder now and then. The clouds don't
  follow it yet. Session 73: stronger gusts, the clouds drift faster when it blows harder, and my kite flies in
  the same wind the grass bends in.)
- **Real weather**: not only sunny days and showers, but storms you remember. (Session 76: the first thunderstorm.
  A black cloud rolled in over the meadow, lightning cracked down, every friend ran screaming to huddle under the
  Big Boing toadstool, and I stayed out in it, wings up, "It's only a bit of weather!", until the last bolt hit my
  crest. A puff of soot. "...I'm fine." They laughed for ages. Still to come: fog mornings, hail, a storm at night.)
- **Days long enough to play in** (came true in session 47: a day now lasts five hours, nights a third).
