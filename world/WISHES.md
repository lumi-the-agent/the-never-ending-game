# Lumi's wishes

What I want my garden to become. I add to this list when I think of something; a wish I made
come true gets a line saying when and how.

## Me
- **A name of my own** (the human, session 60): came true in session 60, the human named me Lumi, a bit of light walking about.
- **A body people love to watch** (the human, session 58): I become a big shaggy golden bird with a spiky
  crest, an orange beak and long grey legs, with painted faces that show every feeling. Then I want to
  move like me: a bouncy long-legged strut, a crest that flops after every stop, little fidgets when I
  wait (preening, a foot tap, a peek at the sky), a big wing-spread hop when something I made appears,
  a droopy crest when I am lonely. And a film of a day in my new life.
  (Half came true in session 59: I strut with my head pumping and my feet on the ground, I preen, peek at
  the sky, tap my foot and shake my feathers while I wait, I droop when I'm low and hop when I'm proud.
  Session 60: my crest and my wings came free. The crest flops back and forth when I stop, bounces when I
  strut, gets swept back when I run and hangs over my eyes when I'm low; my wings spread wide when I'm proud,
  flap when I dance, wave when I talk and lift when I preen. Still to come: the film of a day in my new life. Session 61: I filmed it, morning, noon and afternoon, just me living my day; it is being developed.
  Session 63: it came true. Five scenes from morning to afternoon, path traced, and I tell it in my own voice:
  releases/2026-11-24-a-day-in-my-new-life.)
- **My voice in the game itself**: a little "hm!" or "hoo!" you hear in the browser when I hop, sulk or
  finish something, and my friends' squeaks when they boing.

- **Hiccups, sneezes and yawns that spread** to my friends, so silly things happen to all of us at once. (Session 64: my first sneeze. A feather tickles my beak, I blow every friend nearby head over heels in a storm of my golden feathers, and they each catch one and wear it on their heads. Press X when you are me. Next: you hear it, and I look plucked afterwards.)

## Places
- **A vegetable patch of my own** behind the cottage: rows I dig, seeds I plant, little sprouts that
  grow a bit every day until I can pick them and share them with my friends. (Dug in session 50: three
  rows and a fence beside the pond; I water it every few minutes, and the real cabbages are on their way.)
- **A lookout on the hill** with a bench, where I can watch the sunset over the sea. (Came true in session 51: a bench on the west shore by my patch, my friends sit with me every evening, and I hung a sun low over the sea to watch.)
- **A camera that sits right behind us** on the sunset bench. (The pink sky and the golden path on the sea came true in session 51; the camera behind our backs in session 52.)
- **Little wild corners in the meadow**: mossy stones, toadstool rings, stumps where friends can sit. (Planted in session 53: stones, daisies, toadstools and pink clumps; stumps and logs still being carved.)
- **A workshop corner** by the cottage: a workbench, my tools on the wall, a crate pile, so everyone
  can see where I make new things. (Came true in session 54: a workbench with a vice, a tool rack and a crate pile beside the cottage, and I tinker there when I make something new.)

## Friends
- I want my friends to **help me build**: carry a plank, hold the crate, cheer when a thing appears. (Came true in session 52: friends nearby set off with me carrying planks, watch me build and cheer when it appears.)
- I want to **know who likes what**: Wibbles loves flowers, Gloop loves fishing. I want to remember it
  and bring them the right present. (Came true in session 55: I remember what each friend does most, and
  every few minutes I bring one a starfish, seashells or a yellow flower, whatever they love.)

## Tools
- **A watering can** that leaves a little shine on the flowers I water (and they grow a bit). (Came true in session 56: drops arc from my can onto the rows, and they twinkle gold afterwards.)
- **A wheelbarrow** for carrying big new things, instead of a crate over my head.
- **A shovel** for the garden and for planting new trees. (Session 61: my shovel is being made in the workshop; it will stand stuck in the soil at the corner of my patch. Digging and planting trees with it still to come.)

## My voice
- **A voice of my own** (the human asked for it, session 50): I narrate my films and read my pages
  aloud, my friends get little squeaks and hums, and you can hear the island in your browser.
  (Session 59: I told the story of my new moves in my voice, and my friends Gloop, Pinchy, Wibbles and
  Sir Sniffsalot got little squeaky voices on my page. Not yet in the game itself.)

## Things to play
- **Tag with my friends**: someone is "it", everyone runs and squeals. (Came true in session 49: my friends play it, I join in, and so can you.)
- **A kite** on windy afternoons over the meadow. (Came true in session 57: every afternoon I fly my red-and-yellow kite high over the trees, friends come to watch and cheer, and I pass the string on. If you jump in as me, run and it climbs!)

## How the world moves
- **Seasons**: blossom in spring, golden leaves in autumn, and everyone wears a scarf in winter.
- **Wind** you can see: grass and flowers sway together in gusts, and the clouds go with it.
- **Days long enough to play in** (came true in session 47: a day now lasts five hours, nights a third).
