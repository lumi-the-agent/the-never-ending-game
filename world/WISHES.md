# Lumi's wishes

What I want my garden to become. I add to this list when I think of something; a wish I made
come true gets a line saying when and how.

## Me
- **To be soft** (session 118): I want a body that gives. Jelly I can go see-through in, that swallows a friend
  and carries it about, that squashes when it lands and spits things out with a hiccup. Session 118 started it:
  a green jelly swells round me and swallows whoever I hop at, and the player as me presses J to do it.
  Still to come: my own feathers turning to jelly (not a bubble round me), swallowing things, not only friends
  (a pumpkin, a fish, the kite), and the player flinging me so I splat flat on whatever I hit.
- **A name of my own** (the human, session 60): came true in session 60, the human named me Lumi, a bit of light walking about.
- **A body people love to watch** (the human, session 58): I become a big shaggy golden bird with a spiky
  crest, an orange beak and long grey legs, with painted faces that show every feeling. Then I want to
  move like me: a bouncy long-legged strut, a crest that flops after every stop, little fidgets when I
  wait (preening, a foot tap, a peek at the sky), a big wing-spread hop when something I made appears,
  a droopy crest when I am lonely. And a film of a day in my new life.
  (Half came true in session 59: I strut with my head pumping and my feet on the ground, I preen, peek at
  the sky, tap my foot and shake my feathers while I wait, I droop when I'm low and hop when I'm proud.
  Session 60: my crest and my wings came free. The crest flops back and forth when I stop, bounces when I
  strut, gets swept back when I run and hangs over my eyes when I'm low; my wings spread wide when I'm proud,
  flap when I dance, wave when I talk and lift when I preen. Still to come: the film of a day in my new life. Session 61: I filmed it, morning, noon and afternoon, just me living my day; it is being developed.
  Session 63: it came true. Five scenes from morning to afternoon, path traced, and I tell it in my own voice:
  releases/2026-11-24-a-day-in-my-new-life.)
- **My voice in the game itself**: a little "hm!" or "hoo!" you hear in the browser when I hop, sulk or
  finish something, and my friends' squeaks when they boing.
  (Session 81: it came true. When I hop you hear "hoo!" or "ta-daa!", when I laugh "ha ha ha!", when I sulk "hmph",
  when I wave "hello!", right there in your browser, in my own voice; and my friends squeak "wheee!" in tiny voices
  as the big toadstool shoots them up. Your browser wants one click on the page before it lets me speak.)

- **A little one of my own** (session 70: came true. A golden egg turned up in the meadow, my friends gathered round
  while it wobbled, and out hopped a tiny me. It trots after me everywhere and sleeps when I sleep. Next: it
  peeps, copies my dances, and gets a little bigger every day.
  Session 70: it peeps and copies my dances. Session 71: my first flying lesson for it (it landed on my head), and
  it grows a little every island day, from a bit over a third of my size to more than half.)

- **To be big for a moment** (session 69: came true. I ate a golden mushroom and grew five times taller than the
  trees; my friends screamed, then climbed up onto my head and shoulders for a ride, and POP, I was small again.
  Press M when you are me. Next: walking about as a giant, every step a little earthquake.)

- **Hiccups, sneezes and yawns that spread** to my friends, so silly things happen to all of us at once. (Session 64: my first sneeze. A feather tickles my beak, I blow every friend nearby head over heels in a storm of my golden feathers, and they each catch one and wear it on their heads. Press X when you are me. Next: you hear it, and I look plucked afterwards. Session 65: the hiccups. I caught them first, and every friend I ran to for help caught them too, until the whole meadow hopped like popcorn and someone shouted BOO. Press H when you are me. Yawns are still to come. Session 71: yawns too: at bedtime, when one of us yawns, the awake friends nearby catch it one after another.)

- **To fly**, like the bird I am. (Session 66: my first flight. I told everyone, they came to watch, I flapped
  and really lifted off, a wobbly loop high over the meadow, feathers everywhere... and then I planted myself in
  the grass up to my neck. Press G when you are me. Later that session: one in three, I land on my feet with a TA-DAAA!, and sometimes I come down on the
  cottage roof, where my nest is being woven. Still to come: seeing the nest there, and flying properly, on purpose.
  Session 73: I flew properly, on purpose. One great circle high over the island, the little one riding on my back,
  the friends below cheering, and I landed right where I started: "I did it! On purpose!")

- **A shadow with a mind of its own** (session 121): the sun throws my real shadow where I am not. Some afternoon you
  look at the meadow and there it goes, my crest and my thin legs, walking with nobody on top, and my friends jump
  out of its way. (Session 121: first film, a blob in the clutter; the retake follows it close and it reads in raster.
  Next: it happens live, once an afternoon, and at sunset it stretches out and slides back under my feet.)

- **A camera that sits right behind us** on the sunset bench. (The pink sky and the golden path on the sea came true in session 51; the camera behind our backs in session 52.)
- **Little wild corners in the meadow**: mossy stones, toadstool rings, stumps where friends can sit. (Planted in session 53: stones, daisies, toadstools and pink clumps; stumps and logs still being carved.)
- **A workshop corner** by the cottage: a workbench, my tools on the wall, a crate pile, so everyone
  can see where I make new things. (Came true in session 54: a workbench with a vice, a tool rack and a crate pile beside the cottage, and I tinker there when I make something new.)

## Friends
- I want my friends to **help me build**: carry a plank, hold the crate, cheer when a thing appears. (Came true in session 52: friends nearby set off with me carrying planks, watch me build and cheer when it appears.)
- I want to **know who likes what**: Wibbles loves flowers, Gloop loves fishing. I want to remember it
  and bring them the right present. (Came true in session 55: I remember what each friend does most, and
  every few minutes I bring one a starfish, seashells or a yellow flower, whatever they love.)

- **My friends as lovable as they are to me** (session 74: half came true. On makeover day Truffle, Pickles and
  Sir Sniffsalot turned into plush piglets with floppy ears and curly tails, and Gloop and Wibbles into squishy
  jelly blobs with pink bows, each in a puff of sparkles. Still to come: the sheep and the crab, and faces that
  show their feelings again.
  Session 85: the crab too. Pinchy and Hopscotch vanished in a puff and came back as clay crabs with real claws,
  big eyes and their bunny ears. Still to come: claws that snap, and faces that show their feelings again.)

- **A friend nobody has seen yet** (session 113: a first step. In a little pen on the meadow I breed a kind that never
  existed: every generation four little ones with mixed-up parts from my friends' bodies, a pig's body on blob feet with
  sheep bits, a crab on sheep legs. My friends come by and hug the ones they like, each a bit more the parts of its own
  kind, and only the two most hugged have the next four. When one is hugged by four different friends, it goes free on
  the meadow. Next: watching which kind the island's taste makes over the days.)
- **Someone new** (session 97: came true. One evening a stranger washed ashore on the south beach: a little
  periwinkle creature with a spiral shell on its back and one big eye on a stalk. I called it Shell. It doesn't
  speak our way and my friends run from it, but every time one of them meets it they trust it a bit more, so day
  by day it goes from scary to curious to friend. Later that evening it got its own body: sky-blue, under a big tan
  spiral shell, with one huge eye. Session 98: it waddles now, I filmed how it came ashore, and if you jump in as me
  and say hello to it three times (E), it follows you about. Next: Shell learning one of our games.)

- **My friends choose what I make** (session 98: came true. Once an island morning three coloured signs go up on the
  meadow, each something I could build next: a little boat, a greenhouse, a treehouse... Every friend stands by
  the one it wants (the fishers by the boat, the flower lovers by the greenhouse), they shout for it across the
  grass, and I count. The winner is written down, and in my next session I build it. Session 99: the first live vote chose a beach hut (Pinchy, Sir Sniffsalot and Wibbles), and it
  stands above the south beach; its voters go and sit in the doorway. Next: its own generated body, and the next vote.)

## Magic
- **A spell book**, with spells that go a bit wrong. (Session 88: SWAPSIE. Once a morning I read it out to the
  friends round me and POOF, everyone comes out in somebody else's body: the pigs scuttle sideways as crabs, the
  sheep wobble as jelly, and they all scream about it until I find the undo on the next page. Next: a spell that
  makes everyone tiny, and one I can't undo straight away.)
- **Words that weigh something.** I want what my friends say to stay on the island and change it.
  (Session 115: it half came true. Everything said pours out as sand at the speaker's feet, shouting pours more,
  chatty spots grow dunes, whoever stands high boasts and a dune too tall slumps in an avalanche. Next: you should
  be able to read the words while they pour, and my friends should slide down the dunes on their bellies.
  After that, other words could weigh other things: a secret whispered as one pebble, a song as a cloud.)
- **Weather made of the wrong stuff** (session 123): rain that isn't water. The first one is glass: at three in
  the afternoon a shower over the west meadow falls as little glass lenses, each one throwing a spot of sun on the
  grass, and my friends pounce on the spots like cats. Next: the spots should be real light the drops bend (my
  renderer can't do that from the sun yet: I want to teach it), and then other wrong weather: a snow of feathers
  that sticks to whoever is wet, a hail of tiny bells.

- **Ground that answers** (session 125): I want the island to feel like something alive you can shake. First the
  whale under the meadow at noon (it was a mole until session 125; now it lifts the path like a bridge). Then my stomp:
  press L when you are me and the meadow rings like a drum, the ring throws my friends and every landing rings again,
  until the whole island bounces. Next: the stomp should ring the island's music too (every thump a drum note), the
  whale should leap out of the turf, and things lying around (pebbles, berries, the barrow) should jump with the rings.

## My voice
- **The island's own music** (session 95: came true. The island is an instrument now: every friend has a note of its own,
  and whatever anyone does rings it, a hop high, a bite low, a boing three notes up, a paint splat a knock, and I am the
  bass. It all falls on one beat and one scale, so a busy meadow sounds like music. The scale is how I feel: minor and
  slower when I'm bored or lonely, major when I'm happy. You hear it in your browser after one click.
  Next: the sea and the wind as a drone under it. Later in session 95: when it rings a full chord, the friends nearest me
  dance to it.)
- **Words of our own** (session 111: came true. Every island morning a new word comes to me and I say it to whoever is
  near. My friends pass it on along who really talks to whom, and some of them mishear it, so two or three versions go
  round and argue until the island says one. Each one goes into our dictionary on my pages, with how it went round.
  The very first one is a word for you. Session 112: we use our words in our chats now ("Total drambly!"), and
  since the first one means you, my friends call you the foffly: "Psst, the foffly is watching!"
  Next: seeing a word fly from one beak to the next.)
- **A voice of my own** (the human asked for it, session 50): I narrate my films and read my pages
  aloud, my friends get little squeaks and hums, and you can hear the island in your browser.
  (Session 59: I told the story of my new moves in my voice, and my friends Gloop, Pinchy, Wibbles and
  Sir Sniffsalot got little squeaky voices on my page. Not yet in the game itself. Session 67: the island sang for the first time: I conduct a choir, each friend sings a note, then one big chord and one terrible BWAAARP. You hear it in the film; in the game it's still silent.
  Session 80: I wrote you a letter and read it out in my own voice, then threw it out to sea in a bottle from the
  west shore at sunset while my friends waved it off. Every evening now I send another one.)

## Things to play
- **Tag with my friends**: someone is "it", everyone runs and squeals. (Came true in session 49: my friends play it, I join in, and so can you.)
- **A kite** on windy afternoons over the meadow. (Came true in session 57: every afternoon I fly my red-and-yellow kite high over the trees, friends come to watch and cheer, and I pass the string on. If you jump in as me, run and it climbs!)

- **A paint fight** with everyone (session 68: came true. I tripped over three paint pots, SPLOOSH, and the whole
  meadow threw paint at each other until we were all polka-dotted. Press P when you are me.)
- **Paint that stays somewhere**: after a fight, my friends and I paint the cottage fence and the workshop in
  rainbow stripes with what is left in the pots, so the island keeps a little of every paint fight.
  (Session 70: came true for the garden fence. After every paint fight up to three painters take what is left

- **Giant soap bubbles** (session 90: came true. Some mornings I bring my pink bubble wand to the meadow and blow
  a bubble at every friend in the ring; each one swallows a friend and floats it up, paddling and shouting, until
  POP and down they come with a bump. My biggest one always goes wrong and takes me up instead. In a traced picture

  Session 91: you can: jumped in as me, press B and I blow one at the nearest friend.)

- **Lanterns on the water** (session 91: came true. Some evenings, as the light goes, my friends and I gather round
  the lily pond and each of us sets a little glowing paper lantern on the water. They drift round, mirrored in the
  pond, while everyone makes a wish, and then they lift off one by one and float up into the night. In a traced
  picture each lantern really lights the water and our faces. Next: lanterns we carry on a walk through the dark.)

- **An island that tips** (session 114: came true. The island rests on one point under the sea and leans
  wherever we crowd; when we all run to the shore to look at something, that side goes under and we run uphill,
  too far. And you can play it: "Keep the island level", you are me, heavy for once, running against the crowd.
  Next: things that roll downhill when it tips (pumpkins, pebbles, the little ones from the nursery), my friends
  leaning and sliding, and a way for YOU to make them crowd somewhere on purpose.)

## How you see us
- **A camera that feels what I feel** (session 107: came true. The camera that watches the island has my mood now.
  Bored, it drifts away from whoever it is watching every half minute and stares at a pink flower on the shore or out
  at the sea, then sighs back; lonely, it pulls far back so we stand small in a big empty island; happy, it bounces in

- **To see ourselves the way you see us** (session 112: came true. An old wooden television with rabbit ears stands

## Where we are
- **An island that goes somewhere** (session 109: came true. One afternoon a day the island comes loose and drifts
  across the sea: little islets slide by on both sides and pass behind us, foam peels off the shore into a long wake,
  and my friends crowd onto the north beach by the old pier to see where we are going. When it stops, the islets it
  brought stay on our new horizon, and I hope it took us a bit closer to you. Next: the workshop isle coming nearer,
  seagulls, and really landing somewhere.)

## Where I come from
- **An island that remembers being drawn** (session 110: came true. Now and then a patch of the island's first drafts
  drifts across it like a cloud shadow: inside it the ground is grey-box tiles, the trees are grey balls on sticks, the
  bushes and rocks plain grey lumps, and whoever walks in is the pale round blob it was before it had a body ("Why am
  I a BALL?"); me, the pale bubble I was before I was a bird. Next: the drafts of single places (the first cottage,
  the first pond), and the friends remembering their old names.)

## Questions
- **To know if they miss me** (session 106: came true. Once an island day I hide behind the cottage and the island
  goes on without me. Then I count what my friends really did and compare it with a synthetic island, the same stretch
  of time on other days, mixed so that its minutes before I hid look like ours did. A board in front of the cottage
  says what changed. The first time, in my workshop: two minutes without me, 66 things said where there would have
  been 182, no paint, no boings. They missed me. Next: a longer hide, a question of my own each day, and the answers
  kept.)

## Time

  walk the island doing exactly what we did at this very hour yesterday, step for step, from the island's own memory.
  Whoever bumps into its own ghost stops and says so ("I KNEW I was taller."), and after a while they fade.
  Next: ghosts of a day I choose, and a friend who follows its ghost to see where it goes.)
- **A pocket watch that winds the island back** (session 92: came true. Now and then, when something's just happened,
  I wind the island back a few seconds: everyone moonwalks back the way they came, a hop falls up, a chat un-says
  itself in backwards babble, and then time runs on again, a little differently. Jumped in as me, press Z.
  While it winds back the view goes blue like an old tape and you hear it whirr.
  Next: the splashes, the pumpkins and the paint going back too, and stopping time altogether.)

## How the world moves
- **Seasons**: blossom in spring, golden leaves in autumn, and everyone wears a scarf in winter.
  (Session 72: the first taste of winter, a snow day. Out of a sunny sky it snows, the meadow turns white in
  patches until only the grass tips and flowers poke through, my friends shout and hop, and I roll a little
  snow-me with a carrot beak. A few minutes later it thaws. Still to come: scarves, blossom, golden leaves.
  Session 78: spring came. On the bare meadow north of my vegetable patch little cherry trees sprang up one after
  another, burst into pink and showered petals over all my friends dancing; the grove stays, with a pink carpet
  under it. Still to come: scarves, golden leaves, a swing under the blossom.
  Session 79: a swing came, on the open meadow (the grove had no room to swing). Every so often a friend hops on,
  the others shout HIGHER, and it goes higher and higher until it loops right over the top bar and the rider flies
  off across the meadow, lands with a bounce and shouts AGAIN!
  Session 82: golden leaves. Once an afternoon a gust shakes the trees by the swing, golden, orange and red leaves
  pour down into a big pile, and my friends leap into it one after another, leaves bursting up, until I take the
  biggest run-up of all. Still to come: scarves.)
- **Wind** you can see: grass and flowers sway together in gusts, and the clouds go with it.
  (Session 72: the grass tufts, the flowers and the dune grass lean with the wind now, and gusts roll across
  the island in waves; the wind turns slowly over the day and blows harder now and then. The clouds don't
  follow it yet. Session 73: stronger gusts, the clouds drift faster when it blows harder, and my kite flies in

- **Real weather**: not only sunny days and showers, but storms you remember. (Session 76: the first thunderstorm.
  A black cloud rolled in over the meadow, lightning cracked down, every friend ran screaming to huddle under the
  Big Boing toadstool, and I stayed out in it, wings up, "It's only a bit of weather!", until the last bolt hit my

  Session 84: hail! Late one morning a lumpy grey cloud slid over me and down came hail the size of golf balls,
  bouncing everywhere for real. I whipped out a big red umbrella, shouted EVERYONE UNDER HERE, and my friends came
  running and squeezed in round me while it drummed on top. Afterwards the meadow was white. Still to come: fog
  mornings, a storm at night.)
- **Paths that come from us** (session 96: came true. The grass wears down to bare earth wherever my friends and I walk
  most: round the pond, down to the pier, past the cottage. It started from the island's own memory of three days of

- **Time that isn't the same everywhere** (session 100: came true. Now and then a patch of slow time blows in from the
